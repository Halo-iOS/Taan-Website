Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

1. Our Privacy Philosophy

At Taan, we believe your data belongs to you. Our business is providing soundscapes and digital health tools, not selling your information. We do not track your usage, monitor your habits, or collect personal analytics.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the absolute minimum required to manage your account:

  • Account Details: If you choose to sign up, we only store your name and email address to identify your subscription and sync your preferences.
  • No Usage Data: We do not collect or store information on how you use the app, which soundscapes you play, or your session frequency.

3. Health & Device Data

Taan may request access to HealthKit or Screen Time APIs to provide personalized insights. All health data is processed locally on your device. We never upload, store, or share your health metrics to our servers or any third parties.

4. Data Security

Your name and email are stored using industry-standard encryption. Since we don't collect sensitive usage data, there is no "digital footprint" of your behavior on our servers to be compromised.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our privacy practices, or would like to request the deletion of your account info, please contact us at support@taan.app
